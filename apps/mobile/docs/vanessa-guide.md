# Homestead: how to use it

A short guide for Vanessa (and Cade). Homestead shows what we can safely spend, asks only the questions that need
one of us, and keeps receipts. It never moves money and never sees a bank password.

## Signing in the first time

1. Open Homestead and tap **Sign in with your passkey**.
2. A page opens at homestead.cadejonescpa.com. LastPass offers your passkey. Tap it.
3. You're back in Homestead. It asks to use Face ID. Say yes.

That's the only time you need the passkey for a while. Homestead asks for it again after 30 days, after a week
without opening the app, or if you sign out.

## Face ID

- Face ID opens the app. If you leave for a few minutes and come back, it asks again.
- It also asks before anything that changes the plan: approving or turning down a budget change, saving your data
  to a file, or turning Face ID off. That's how Homestead knows it was really you.
- If Face ID doesn't work, tap **Try Face ID again**, or use your phone's passcode.

## The Home screen

- **The big number** is what's safe to spend until the next payday. Bills due before payday and money set aside
  (the emergency fund, Christmas, car) are already taken out. Tap it to see the parts.
- **On track, Watch, Action needed** tell you how things look. If money is short before payday, Homestead says how
  short and suggests one small move. Nothing turns red and shouts.
- Under every number is a line like **"Data through Oct 19 · 2 items pending."** That's how fresh the numbers are
  and how many purchases are still being recorded.

## Snapping a receipt (three taps)

1. Tap the green **Snap** button at the bottom.
2. Hold the receipt in the frame. It snaps by itself when the phone is steady, or tap the big white button.
3. Check the card and tap **Done**.

That's it. Homestead reads the receipt and matches it to the charge when the charge shows up from the bank,
usually in a day or two. Walmart, club-store and Amazon receipts of $25 or more also split themselves into the right
categories.

**"Saved on your phone — not synced yet"** means you snapped it without a connection. The receipt is safe on the
phone and goes up by itself as soon as you're back online. A bar at the top says how many are waiting. You don't
need to do anything, and snapping the same receipt twice won't count it twice.

## Answering questions

**Needs your answer** shows at most three cards. Each one says why it's asking. One tap answers it:

- "Walmart $142.37 — split from your receipt. Look right?" **Yes**, or **Change** to pick something else.
- "Venmo +$85 from Kayla — lash appointment?" **Yes, lash**, or **Something else**.
- "Amazon $46.18 — what was it?" Tap the closest category.

When nothing needs you, the card says so.

## When something is wrong

Open **Activity**, tap the item, tap **This is wrong**, and pick the right category (five suggestions, or search).
Turn on **Do this every time** if it should always go that way; Cade approves the rule first.

- If it hasn't reached the books yet, it's fixed right away.
- If it's already in the books, it shows **Fix pending** and Cade fixes it there.

**Mark as business** is for the rare purchase that was for Cade's firm (CTJ) or for lash supplies. Everything else
stays personal without asking.

## What the words mean

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Needs a quick answer | One tap from you or Cade and it's done |
| Recorded | Homestead has it; it's on its way to the books |
| In the books | Cade's books have it |
| Matched to the bank | The bank statement agrees. Finished |
| Being checked | Something didn't line up; Cade is looking |
| Not synced yet | A receipt still on your phone, waiting for a connection |

## Budget and moving money

**Budget** shows each category with what's left. "Sample budget until ours is approved" means the numbers are
examples for now.

To move money between categories, tap the category you want to add to, choose the amount and where it comes from,
and tap **Send to Cade**. Nothing changes until he approves. When Cade asks you, the request shows on Home and on
Budget: **Approve with Face ID**, or **Not now**.

## Goals, Bills and Ask Homestead

- **Goals** shows the emergency fund and the next debt to pay off. Targets say "sample" until we approve them.
- **Bills** (from Home, "All bills") lists what's due in the next few weeks, with payday marked.
- **Ask Homestead** answers plain questions like "How much have we spent eating out?" Every answer says what date
  its numbers run through. If you ask it to move money, it drafts the request for you to send; it never moves money
  itself.

## Settings

Tap your initial at the top right: Face ID on or off, notifications, quiet hours (9 pm to 7 am; only an emergency
gets through at night), the bank logins and whether they're connected, **Export my data**, and **Sign out**.
