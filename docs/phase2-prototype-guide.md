# Phase 2 prototype — how to try it

The prototype is a single web page with example numbers. Nothing is connected to a real account and nothing it shows is our real balance. Open it on a phone from the link Cade shares; it also works in a desktop browser (it sits in a phone-width column).

## For Vanessa

1. Open the link and tap your name. Tap **Turn on Face ID** (it's pretend here; the real app uses your phone's Face ID).
2. Look at the first screen. Does "safe to spend" make sense without anyone explaining it? Tap the big number to see where it comes from.
3. Answer the three questions under **Needs your answer**. One tap each.
4. Tap the green **Snap** button. It takes a sample receipt by itself; **Use my camera** takes a real photo if you'd like to try that.
5. Turn on Airplane Mode and snap another receipt. It should say "saved on your phone — not synced yet", then catch up when you turn Airplane Mode off.
6. In **Activity**, open anything and tap **This is wrong** to change its category.
7. In **Budget**, approve Cade's request, then tap a category and send him a change of your own.
8. In **Goals**, see whether the two bars tell you what you'd want to know.
9. Tap the **Ask Homestead** bar and ask a question in your own words.
10. Tap the **V** at the top right to see settings. The **Scene** switch there shows how the first screen reads when money is tight.

Then tell Cade: would you open this most days? What was confusing? What would you never use?

## What Gate 2 measures

- Snap a receipt from the home screen in three taps or fewer.
- "Safe to spend before the next paycheck" is understood at a glance, including the parts.
- Each question is answerable in one tap and says why it's asked.
- A wrong category can be fixed in two taps.
- Offline capture shows "not synced yet" and syncs on reconnect.
- Nothing on screen reads as accounting software.

## What the prototype does not do

- No real bank data, OCR, Business Central, or notifications. Answers from Ask Homestead are canned from the sample data.
- Face ID and approvals are simulated. Export is a placeholder.
- The sample budget is marked as such; the real budget is being built separately.

Prototype settings keep your taps on your own phone only. **Reset the prototype** (in Settings) starts over.
